# Sales Data Analysis Project



### Project Overview

This project analyzes sales data to identify business trends, evaluate product and regional performance, understand customer segments, and examine profitability. The goal is to turn raw sales data into meaningful insights that can support data-driven business decisions.



### Objectives

\- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.

\- Identify monthly and yearly sales trends.

\- Compare revenue and profit across product categories.

\- Evaluate regional performance.

\- Analyze customer segments and top customers.

\- Identify top-performing products.

\- Understand the relationship between discounts and profit margins.

\- Analyze shipping duration and data quality.

### 

### Tools and Technologies

\- Excel: Data inspection and preparation

\- MySQL: Data analysis using SQL queries, aggregations, grouping, and subqueries

\- Python: Data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and visualization

\- Pandas: Data manipulation and analysis

\- Matplotlib: Data visualization

\- Power BI: Interactive dashboard development (planned/in progress)



### Dataset

The dataset contains 1,200 sales records and 16 original columns, including:



\- Order ID and order dates

\- Customer ID and customer segment

\- Sales channel and region

\- Product ID, product name, and category

\- Quantity and unit price

\- Discount, sales, and profit

\- Shipping date



### Analysis Performed



###### 1\. Sales Performance

Analyzed total revenue, profit, transactions, and sales trends over time.



###### 2\. Product Analysis

Compared categories and products by revenue, profit, units sold, and profit margin.

###### 

###### 3\. Regional Analysis

Compared sales, profit, transaction volume, and profit margins across regions.



###### 4\. Customer Analysis

Evaluated customer segments and identified the highest-revenue customers.



###### 5\. Discount Analysis

Compared discount levels with revenue, profit, and profit margins.

###### 

###### 6\. Shipping Analysis

Calculated shipping duration and compared average delivery time across regions.



###### 7\. Data Quality

\- Checked for missing values and duplicate records.

\- Validated date columns.

\- Checked sales calculations against the discount formula.

\- Checked for non-positive sales, quantity, and unit prices.

\- Checked for negative shipping durations.



### Key Findings



\- Technology generated the highest revenue and profit among product categories.

\- East was the leading region by revenue and profit.

\- Small Business was the strongest customer segment by revenue and profit.

\- Laptop was the top product by revenue and profit.

\- The 0% discount group had the highest total revenue, total profit, and profit margin among the discount groups analyzed.

\- Average shipping duration was approximately 3.95 days.

\- The highest monthly revenue occurred in May 2025, while the highest monthly profit occurred in December 2024.
